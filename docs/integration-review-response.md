# Response to the integration-test review

The review assessed commit `0274d2c`. It is useful evidence about the foundation, not a request to implement the entire integration suite in this patch. The intentionally small initial feature set and the decision to postpone unattended promotion remain in force.

Diagnostics and status are fetched through nginx. Accelerated scheduling exposed starvation; oldest-due-first dispatch now prevents it, with a focused regression. Ready status uses the non-secret marker published after networking accepts a configuration. |

This deliberate interruption makes the existing-file branch observable without changing production routing semantics, job bodies or quality profiles. The test verifies the persisted `hadFiles` intent, exact bytes at the destination and no remaining staging payload.

## Policy and earlier architectural issues

Synthetic peers were explicitly requested and authorized during this campaign. The updated repository constraint records that decision; another approval is unnecessary. All peers remain synthetic and confined to verified isolated namespaces. Host networking is untouched.

The stale AGENTS.md reference to removed real-engine suites is corrected. PR/full-mode removal is intentional: the previous broad disconnected matrices were removed before adding application workflows. The backlog above makes the temporary coverage reduction explicit.

The recommendation to restore a manual HAOS release gate conflicts with the user's decision. It is not restored. Supervisor installation, actual HAOS ingress/authentication, AppArmor, NAS permissions/hardlinks and Plex playback remain documented unautomated boundaries. Native nginx diagnostics in this test do not establish HA ingress authorization. Unattended dependency promotion and release remain disabled pending expanded acceptance.

Of the earlier 12 issues: 1/2/5/6 gain DNS, process-tree, Ready, clean-stop and CI-clone evidence here; 8 gains same-version existing-state boot only; 12 gains continued local service and reconnect evidence, not cold-start-without-VPN coverage. Issues 3/7/10 retain the explicitly listed missing failure-isolation, closure and direct-grab scenarios. Issues 4/9/11 remain resolved by removing runtime swaps, vendoring apps and limiting support to amd64. There is no new downgrade, legacy-migration or HA restore guarantee.

## Validation

`bash tests/run.sh --layout ci` passed locally with host Podman: 9 product logic checks, 9 harness checks, 8 required packet tests, four shared real movie scenarios, a real VPN fault/reconnect and one configured-clone scenario. Total with cached layers: 132.61 seconds. Both HTTP/DNS unexpected-request lists were empty. The earlier focused fresh/outage/clone check also passed; timing and coverage limits are recorded in [testing.md](testing.md). Docker/HAOS AppArmor and real Supervisor/NAS/Plex behavior are not claimed as locally validated.

The shared-invariant follow-up measured 123.71 seconds for the full CI layout (104.50 seconds integration), versus 132.61/113.72 before consolidation and cleanup overlap. Ordinary checkpoints cost about 0.1 seconds. All five scenarios finished with cleanup verified; controller/source evidence and owned native command history remain cumulative. Exact scope, regressions and timing limits are documented in the testing guide.

The first integration coverage expansion and remaining boundaries are recorded in [the numbered scenario catalogue](integration-scenarios.md) and [testing guide](testing.md). Active assertions are not a claim that every new product behavior currently passes.

The expanded focused CI cohort passed twelve reported scenarios (eleven shared outcomes plus a configured-clone control) in 265.70 seconds with cached layers, including 11 product logic, 13 harness and 8 required packet checks. The separate in-flight VPN-loss assertion currently fails its 60-second download-completion wait after successful tunnel recovery. It remains required in default acceptance; see the testing guide for the observation, reproduction command and unresolved diagnosis. The passing focused cohort does not establish full release readiness. Approved Seerr requests cannot be declined through its public API; completed-content closeout uses deletion, and genuinely pending-request decline remains stretch coverage.
